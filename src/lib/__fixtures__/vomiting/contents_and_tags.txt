VOMITING SAQ SETS — CONTENTS AND TAGS

This index contains diagnostic spoilers. Use all_questions.txt for practice.
Each set has a separate question file and explained-answer file.

VOM-01: Acute vomiting and diarrhoea — 23 marks
Coverage: Gastroenteritis/foodborne illness, Dehydration, Sepsis, Basic fluid treatment,
Antiemetics and adverse effects

VOM-02: Vomiting food several hours after meals — 23 marks
Coverage: Gastroparesis, Gastric outlet obstruction, Diabetes, Medication effects,
Metoclopramide

VOM-03: Blood in the vomit — 25 marks
Coverage: Peptic ulcer bleeding, Varices, Mallory–Weiss tear, Bleeding severity, Oesophageal
rupture, PPI, Alcohol withdrawal, Thiamine

VOM-04: Vomiting with weakness and low blood pressure — 25 marks
Coverage: Adrenal crisis, Sepsis/pyelonephritis, Hypoglycaemia, Hyponatraemia, Hypercalcaemia,
Uraemia, Steroid withdrawal, Wernicke encephalopathy, Hepatic encephalopathy

VOM-05: Vomiting with headache — 23 marks
Coverage: Migraine, Meningitis, Subarachnoid haemorrhage, Raised intracranial pressure, Basic
neurological assessment, Antiemetic pharmacology

ALCOHOL CROSSOVER TAGS

Use "alcohol abuse" as your umbrella tag if that matches your existing deck.
An alternative umbrella label is "alcohol use disorder".
A tag indicates topic overlap; it does not establish the cause in a patient.

For Q2–5, retain the stem or the relevant short scenario when making a card.
Bleeding and oesophageal rupture also have causes unrelated to alcohol.
This is a vomiting bank with alcohol overlap, not a complete alcohol curriculum.

LEVEL AND MARKING

One fact per mark. If a reason is requested, it receives its own mark.
Explanations in the answer files are optional learning material.
No drug doses, withdrawal scores, complex fluid calculations, antibiotic schedules,
specialist intervention choices or exact eTG protocols are examined.

SOURCE APPROACH
