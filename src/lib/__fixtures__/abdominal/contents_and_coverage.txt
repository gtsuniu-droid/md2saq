DESIGN
The difficulty comes from prioritisation, discrimination between common diseases,
changing the differential as findings emerge, and explaining anatomy and management.
Each set retains a 9-mark differential/history/examination question in the requested format.
Investigation selection/interpretation is focused, not the majority of the assessment.
The explanations provide learning detail without requiring essays for small mark items.

These are original educational drafts, not formally calibrated examination items.
