Additional explanation questions cover adrenal insufficiency, cardiac outflow obstruction,
hyperventilation physiology, ototoxic vestibular loss, test limitations and coexisting disease.
Exact drug doses and specialist criteria are not required. Functional dizziness is tested
through clinical reasoning and a supplied diagnostic explanation rather than rare-label recall.

The cases are educational drafts, not formally calibrated or validated examination items.
