# SAQMD2 Code of Conduct

[Project](https://github.com/gtsuniu-droid/md2saq) · [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Apache 2.0 licence](LICENSE)

## Our Commitment

Welcome to the SAQMD2 community.

SAQMD2 is committed to building an open, collaborative and inclusive community where people can contribute, learn, share ideas and improve the project together.

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds, identities, experience levels and perspectives.

Whether you are reporting a bug, suggesting a feature, contributing code, improving documentation or participating in a discussion, we expect everyone to treat each other with respect.

Our community is guided by three principles.

## 1. Be Thoughtful and Respectful

We treat others with patience, kindness and professionalism.

We recognise that contributors bring different experiences, skills, cultures and perspectives.

We encourage everyone to:

- Welcome new contributors and help them participate.
- Respect differences in opinions, backgrounds and experience.
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intentions while remaining accountable for outcomes.
- Communicate clearly and considerately.
- Give credit where it is due.

Technical expertise does not excuse disrespectful behaviour.

## 2. Disagree Constructively

Open-source projects improve through discussion, challenge and debate.

Disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

When reviewing contributions or discussing decisions:

- Focus on ideas, evidence and technical merit.
- Explain disagreements clearly and respectfully.
- Critique the work rather than the person.
- Remain open to alternative approaches.
- Respect maintainers' decisions, even when you disagree.
- Avoid turning technical discussions into personal conflicts.

Strong opinions are welcome when expressed respectfully.

## 3. Take Responsibility and Learn

Everyone makes mistakes. What matters is how we respond.

When a concern is raised, we expect community members to listen, reflect and take reasonable steps to resolve the issue.

This includes:

- Acknowledging mistakes.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Correcting inaccurate or misleading information.
- Addressing unintended harm.
- Working towards a respectful resolution.

Our goal is to maintain a community where people feel comfortable contributing and raising concerns.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not tolerated:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation or threats.
- Discriminatory or derogatory language, jokes or comments.
- Personal insults, repeated hostility or targeted attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualised language or imagery.
- Deliberately disrespecting someone's name, pronouns or identity.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Stalking, unwanted contact or invasive monitoring.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, reviews or community activities.
- Encouraging others to engage in harassment or abusive behaviour.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Reasonable criticism of technical decisions, code quality, security or project governance does not constitute harassment merely because it is critical or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all SAQMD2 community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Project documentation and contribution channels.
- Community forums and online communication platforms.
- Project-related meetings, events and collaborative activities.
- Public interactions where someone is officially representing SAQMD2.

It applies equally to contributors, maintainers, reviewers and other community participants.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that may violate this Code of Conduct, please contact the SAQMD2 maintainers privately.

**Reporting contact:** [chagc@student.unimelb.edu.au](mailto:chagc@student.unimelb.edu.au)

Please include, where possible:

- A description of what happened.
- When and where the incident occurred.
- Relevant links, messages or screenshots.
- Names of those involved or potential witnesses.
- Any immediate safety concerns.

You are not required to confront the person involved before reporting an incident.

Please avoid posting sensitive reports as public GitHub issues.

Reports will be treated seriously and handled discreetly, with information shared only where reasonably necessary to assess and resolve the matter.

Where possible, reports involving a maintainer should be reviewed by someone who is not directly involved.

## Resolution and Enforcement

SAQMD2 maintainers are responsible for reviewing reported violations and taking appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances and severity, actions may include:

1. **Guidance:** A private discussion explaining the concern and expected behaviour.
2. **Formal warning:** A clear warning with conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporary limits on commenting, contributing or participating.
4. **Permanent removal:** A ban from SAQMD2 community spaces.

Severe incidents may warrant immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Decisions will consider the circumstances, seriousness, evidence and any pattern of behaviour.

Where appropriate, affected individuals will be informed of the outcome, subject to privacy and confidentiality considerations.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to uphold this Code of Conduct and set an example through their own conduct.

They may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary.

Maintainers should make moderation decisions fairly and consistently and avoid conflicts of interest.

This Code of Conduct does not guarantee acceptance of any contribution. Technical and project governance decisions remain the responsibility of maintainers.
